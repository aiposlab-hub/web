One order, every screen

Follow one order
through Carte.

See how a single order travels from the table tablet to the kitchen, the floor, the bill and tonight's report.

  1. 1

    Guests order at the table.

    Guests add dishes, pick add-ons and send the order from the tablet on their table. Servers spend less time taking orders and more time with guests.

    See Table Order
  2. 2

    The kitchen gets the ticket instantly.

    The ticket lands on the kitchen display the moment it is sent, next to the online and delivery orders. Cooks tap each one from New to Prep, Cooking and Ready.

    See Kitchen Display
  3. 3

    Servers see every table.

    Every table shows whether it is occupied and for how long. Tap a table to see exactly what was ordered.

    See the POS
  4. 4

    The bill is paid, split any way.

    Card, cash or points. Pay the full amount, or split by item, by amount or by payer on the same screen.

    See the POS
  5. 5

    The report is ready at close.

    Sales by hour, tax, tips and best sellers are in the report the moment the bill closes. No spreadsheet.

    See the back office
