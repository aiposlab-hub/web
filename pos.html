POS

Everything a shift needs,
on one screen.

Orders come in from tables, the counter, your website and the delivery apps. On Carte they all land in one list, and everything after that happens on the same screen.

  • Every order in one list, whatever channel it came from
  • Each server signs in with their own passcode
  • Change calculated as soon as cash is entered
  • Membership points at checkout, refunds by original payment

Everything else is on the home screen.

Look up a past transaction, check today's tips, log cash in and out, sell a gift card or print a report. No back-office login needed.
